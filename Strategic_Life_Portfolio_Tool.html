Why these 7 questions work

Corporate strategy has evolved over decades into a rigorous, repeatable process. This framework translates that discipline to personal life.

By answering the same questions organizations use to allocate capital and talent, you create clarity, identify misalignments, and make deliberate portfolio choices about how you spend your finite time and energy.

The assessment step (question 4) is the most actionable: it turns vague feelings of “life balance” into a data-driven view you can act on immediately.
