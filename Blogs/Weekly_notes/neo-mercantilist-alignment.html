Where it's playing out

Global

The core claim, per Every/Taggart's 27 August note: nations are effectively being forced onto US rails — markets, tech, finance, stablecoins — or toward Iran, with China not cleanly fitting either camp. Stablecoins double as a sanctions tool in this framing, not just a payments innovation. The Treasury "twist" — funding long-end buybacks with bill issuance — is presented as statecraft that will likely be tried again, not a one-off.

Iran / Venezuela

Diesel exports and diesel stock/crack spreads are the physical-constraint tell on this thread — this map treats diesel, refining capacity, copper, and grid buildout as binding before any financial engineering does. Venezuelan crude production, moving slowly against its prior peak, is the secondary indicator on the same theme.

Europe

The 21 September 2026 Duma election date is flagged specifically as a Russia/NATO/Iran-missile volatility tail for this thread — separate from the Europe/UK reserve-asset story covered in the rails-and-gold piece.

United States — grid politics

Interconnection queue megawatts and electricity CPI are where the AI-capex story and the alignment story physically intersect: data centers raise local power bills unless communities share in the gains, and that political friction is itself part of the binding constraint on how fast this alignment thesis can actually scale.

What this means to trade

Individuals

Treat human-capital positioning — skills that pair with AI rather than compete with the healthcare-admin-cut channel — as the personal-level version of "choosing a rail." The same alignment logic applies at the individual labor-market level.

Companies

Energy, grid and industrial firms: treat copper and refining capacity as use-assets, not commodities to trade on price alone. Building AI infrastructure firms should contract power and share bill relief with host communities proactively — the permit, not the financing, is what kills a project when that step is skipped.

Nation-states

Treat energy, refining, copper and grid equipment explicitly as reserves. If issuing debt, stay bill-heavy rather than assuming any single ceiling permanently pins long-end rates. Budget alignment access to US rails as deliberately as any currency reserve.
