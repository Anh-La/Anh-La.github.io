KEY GLOBAL FINDINGS — LAST 100+ YEARS
CYCLE LENGTH
Credit cycles: 8–20+ years

Significantly longer than business cycles (2–8 yrs). Median credit boom ~10 years.

DEFAULTS
Highly clustered & persistent

Long-run avg ~1.5%. Worst clusters (1870s) exceeded Great Depression in severity. Only ~26% correlation with NBER recessions.

SYNCHRONIZATION
Rose sharply post-1970s

Financial globalization increased co-movement across advanced economies. No single global driver.

LEVERAGE IMPACT
Credit booms predict deeper recessions

Strong evidence across 14+ countries since 1870. Financial-crisis recessions cost more in output loss.
