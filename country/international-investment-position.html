Net IIP = Assets − Liabilities · millions of USD · end-of-period stocks

The International Investment Position is the balance sheet of an economy's external claims and obligations: the stock of foreign assets held by residents against the stock of liabilities owed to non-residents, broken out by direct investment, portfolio investment, financial derivatives, other investment, and reserve assets.
