Elasticity-based Forecast (2014)
① Δ%Prxf = ((1 + Δ%Pxf/100) × (1 + Δ%NER/100) / (1 + Δ%CPI/100) − 1) × 100
② Δ%RXg = εRX,y × Δ%RYp + εRX,Prxf × Δ%Prxf
③ Xg₂₀₁₄ = Xg₂₀₁₃ × (1 + Δ%RXg/100) × (1 + Δ%Pxf/100)
④ Mg₂₀₁₄ = Mg₂₀₁₃ × (1 + Δ%RMg/100) × (1 + Δ%Pmf/100)
⑤ Services Credit = Xg × service_credit_ratio · Services Debit = Mg × service_debit_ratio
⑥ Investment income = yield × avg foreign assets · Interest payments = (LIBOR + spread) × avg external debt
